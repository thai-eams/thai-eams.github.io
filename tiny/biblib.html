SYNAP [home/lab] and TEAMS presents: bib.lib Listening Session

28 November 2026
Bibliothek, Goethe-Institut Thailand
18/1 Sathon 1 Alley, Lane 2, Thung Maha Mek, Sathon, Bangkok 10120


© Thai ElectroAcoustic Music Society (TEAMS)

"bib.lib" is a listening session held by the Thai ElectroAcoustic Music Society inside the Goethe-Institut Bangkok's Bibliothek, as part of the "Siang / เสียง" festival. Inside the Bibliothek, loudspeakers are scattered across the library and arranged in unconventional manners, often behind bookshelves, on the table, or under the listeners, to create a faux surround sound system in guise of a 'listening room'. Music performed in this listening session are all in stereo, curated and spatialized by Sippapas Thienwiwat.

About the Host

Sippapas Thienwiwat

Sippapas Thienwiwat (b. 2006) is a Bangkok-based composer, digital artist, scholar, and curator. His music and sound arts challenge the notion of post-acousmaticism by adopting technology as raw compositional material. His works have been presented in the USA, England, Ireland, Germany, Brazil, Ecuador, Hong Kong, Singapore, and Thailand. Recent collaborations include BEAST, CEMI, EMuSE, OSSIA New Music, Sonic Officer, and TACET(i) Ensemble. He is the President of the Thai ElectroAcoustic Music Society (TEAMS), currently under the tutelage of Thanapat Ogaslert, Jiradej Setabundhu, Scott Wilson, and Matthias Jung.
